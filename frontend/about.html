THE IDEA BEHIND BMW JOURNEY

More Than A Drive.

BMW Journey is a simple platform designed to record, track, and remember every road you travel.

ABOUT THE PROJECT

Your road. Your story.

Every road has a destination, but the journey is what creates the memory.

BMW Journey provides a digital space where drivers can record their journeys using distance and GPS coordinates.

The goal is simple: make it easy to keep a personal record of the roads you've travelled.
