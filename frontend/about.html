THE IDEA BEHIND BMW JOURNEY

More Than A Drive.

BMW Journey is a precision telemetry platform designed to record, track, and remember every road you travel.

ABOUT THE PROJECT

Your road. Your story.

Every road has a destination, but the journey is what creates the memory.

BMW Journey provides a digital space where drivers can seamlessly record their journeys using live distance calculations and precise GPS coordinates.

The goal is simple: make it effortless to maintain a permanent personal logbook of the roads you've conquered.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

Built for every journey.

01

Track Journeys

Record the exact distance travelled during your drive and maintain an organized history of your complete driving logs.

02

GPS Location

Capture real-time latitude and longitude coordinates to pinpoint the start and end of your trips accurately.

03

Journey History

View past telemetry data, average metrics, and route locations straight from a centralized data table.

04

Simple Dashboard

Monitor your lifetime aggregate distance and total completed trips through a clean, modern interface.
